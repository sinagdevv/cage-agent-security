# CAGE Research & Experiments

This directory houses the academic, conceptual, and empirical research foundations of CAGE.

## Subdirectories

- [`threat_model/`](threat_model/): Formalized threat vectors, attacker capabilities, and trust boundary definitions.
- [`experiments/`](experiments/): Experimental protocols, hypotheses, and empirical evaluations.
- [`benchmarks/`](benchmarks/): Benchmark suites for latency, policy complexity, and detection efficacy.
